2. **Headline + About are the prompt.** They are the first ~700 tokens an AI sourcing agent ingests when ranking you. Lead with role-fit signals and quantified proof.

Add to that: real photo, "Open to Work" recruiter-only signal, and active posting on your build-in-public projects — these compound weekly.

## 8. Activity strategy (this is what compounds)

The 2026 algorithm weights **"depth score"** — recent meaningful engagement on your topic cluster — heavily. A profile with no posts ranks below an equally-skilled profile that posts twice a week.

### Minimum viable cadence
- **2 posts/week** in your topic cluster (agents, MCP, Claude, voice AI, build-in-public).
- **1 long post or article/month** — pick from your existing repos. Each repo README is a draft post: AgentMesh, OllamaBar, the 25-agent org, the InterviewAgent architecture.
- **Comment on 5 posts/week** from people in your target companies.

### Post types that perform
1. **Build-in-public updates with screenshots/GIFs** — your 6 active projects are a year of content.
2. **"Lessons from shipping X"** — short, opinionated, 1 specific takeaway.
3. **Repo launches** — link in first comment (LinkedIn de-prioritizes link posts).
4. **Architecture diagrams** — your ARCHITECTURE-AUDIT.md is one post per section.

### What to avoid
- Generic "10 things I learned about AI" listicles — depth-score punishes these.
- Reposts without commentary.
- AI-written posts with no personal voice — the algorithm flags overly-generic text now.
